## Truth core

- `SITE-ANDOR-WHISPERS-CONTACT` exists in Andor old city / theatre-street social ecology under the public name **曲聞會館**.
- Publicly, 曲聞會館 is a performance, story, message-exchange and private-instructor referral venue used by performers, patrons and people looking for introductions.
- Hidden function: the venue is also a **local contact / screening / teaching-network node** used by some members or associates of the Low Whisper College.
- This is a local node, **not established as the Low Whisper headquarters**. Exact headquarters location remains unresolved and may be source-resolved or generated later if relevant.
- Not every employee, performer or patron at 曲聞會館 belongs to Low Whisper College.
- Ordinary visitors can reach and use the public venue without knowing the hidden affiliation.
- A visitor seeking bardic instruction can openly request a private instructor referral. Requests suggesting interest in information work, discreet patronage, secrets, court/power networks or Low Whisper itself can be routed to an affiliated intermediary if that intermediary judges engagement useful/safe.
- Directly saying “Low Whisper College” does not force the staff to confess affiliation. It can produce an ordinary public response, a denial, deflection, or a private follow-up depending on the staff member's actual knowledge/goals at that moment.
- If a specific intermediary becomes observable or their reaction materially affects a roll, create/commit that actor's minimum identity/knowledge/goal/constraints before the reaction/roll.
