## Public structure

- A modest public chapel and an adjacent dispensary operate as separate but cooperating doors in the same south-district care network.
- Ordinary emergency injuries, fever, wound cleaning, simple medicines and bandaging are first routed to the dispensary.
- Prayer, grief, family tracing, temporary social support, burial/rite questions and people who simply need someone to speak with are first routed to the chapel.
- Mixed cases are routinely handed between the two; this is ordinary public practice, not a hidden organization.
- Immediate dangerous cases are not refused solely for lack of money; ordinary medicine/material costs may still be charged or donation-supported depending on means.
- More specialized magical, curse-like, toxic or otherwise unusual cases may be referred onward to a more capable priest/healer/institution when local staff cannot safely handle them. Exact referral targets are case-dependent and not globally precommitted.
